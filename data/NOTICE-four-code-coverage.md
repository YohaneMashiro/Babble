# Four-code coverage supplement

Source/adoption date: 2026-10-28. This supplement separates availability of a verified code from availability of its root explanation. It does not modify the original canonical datasets or their source locks.

Published full codes are selected, without creating or repairing codes, from OverflowCat/libre-flypy 1.2.0's [published CC0 table](https://github.com/OverflowCat/libre-flypy/releases/tag/1.2.0). The exact original input and its CC0 statement are already preserved by `data/shape-sources.lock.json` and `data/NOTICE-shape-traces.md`. Current modern readings and the Xiaohe sound scheme retain `NOTICE-Unicode.txt` and `NOTICE-shuangpin-practice.txt`. Every retained reading/code pair must independently match its sound prefix and the fixed external reference. No root names are derived from code letters; such records have no claimed endpoints.

The separately authored stroke selection for 平 is in `data/curated-shape-supplement.json`. Its original explanatory prose and selection metadata are MIT, under LICENSE-CODE. Underlying stroke facts are from TJYSunset/Strokes.txt at `f993e6d7cd348e34d35efda29e356c258fb277c5` (WTFPL), retained in `tools/shape-expansion/inputs` with its upstream notice. Positional root-to-key definitions are from the existing AGPL-3.0-only libre-flypy root map. The derived curated root record retains AGPL-3.0-only and those input notices; MIT prose does not relicense it. Stroke indices identify source facts, not a generic geometric proof or complete teaching decomposition.

The official rules page is linked as a factual rule reference, with an original explanation rather than copied tutorial text. The official JSON is an external, fixed comparison-only validator. Neither its raw payload nor its independent decomposition fields are redistributed. Reference agreement is not independent teaching accuracy, and the source table's remaining unmatched/unchecked long tail is not promoted by this supplement.

Generator, program/input hashes, derived bytes, counts and comparison SHA256 are recorded in `data/four-code-coverage.lock.json`. The generator first constructs published/curated candidates from their independent inputs, then opens the comparison snapshot. Normal offline checking needs no external official file. Source, generator, original inputs, notices and derived records are included in the corresponding-source archive.
