## External rules and accuracy limits

[Official rules](https://flypy.cc/help/#/gz) and [official root explanations](https://flypy.cc/help/#/zg) are factual references for original short paraphrases and case-specific checks. Their original Markdown, images, official answer tables and full root map are not distributed; no open license for those originals is asserted. The research handoff records the external snapshot hashes. Official lookup data remains an external reject/accept validator, not a source copied into these records.

The reviewed set contains 13 cards: 11 root-selection or stroke-category sequences, and 2 endpoint/context records (鹤、健). Even sequence scope means a reviewed teaching selection, not verified vector geometry or a stroke-order animation. Endpoint scope exposes an unexpanded region. Other characters retain a pending-analysis state. The root-use inventory is partial and position-specific (`complete: false`); it does not establish a full official root table or turn Chai trial output into training data.

The HY review list has 24 characters / 82 occurrences, 14 endpoint-derivation gaps and 10 named-component gaps. It includes no official code, root, decomposition, reading answer or Chai candidate. The categories describe evidence still needed, not authorization to fill answers by similarity.
